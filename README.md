  <h1>🥤 Coca-Cola Sales Dashboard (Power BI)</h1>
      <p class="lead">An end-to-end Power BI project to explore sales performance, operating profit, and geographic trends for a beverage portfolio. Includes slicers, KPI cards, drilldowns, and a Bing map with zoom controls.</p>
      <div class="badges">

  <h2>📌 Overview</h2>
      <p>This dashboard summarizes sales for multiple beverage brands (Coca-Cola, Dasani Water, Diet Coke, Powerade, Sprite, Fanta) across time and states. It helps answer:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><b>What is total sales</b> over a selected invoice-date period?</li>
        <li>Which <b>brands drive operating profit</b> and at what margin?</li>
        <li>How do <b>units sold</b> and <b>price per unit</b> trend by brand?</li>
        <li>Which <b>states contribute most to revenue</b> (with a zoomable map)?</li>
      </ul>
